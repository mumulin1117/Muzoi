Community Rules

Your creativity. Our shared space.

A few essentials before you join.

18+ community

Adults only. Always respectful.

01 Be kind. Stay respectful. No bullying, hate, harassment or unwanted contact.

Celebrate the artist behind the look. Give thoughtful feedback and respect different styles, identities and skill levels.

Bullying, body-shaming, harassment, hate speech, discrimination and threats have no place here.

02 Create freely. Share responsibly. Post work you own. Protect privacy and keep content safe.

Share makeup, artistic transformations and beauty inspiration. Keep SFX looks clearly creative; avoid graphic gore, realistic depictions of serious injury or content intended to shock.

No pornography, sexual exploitation, hateful or illegal content, or material that endangers minors. Never promote violence or self-harm.

03 Protect your peace. Report harmful content. Block unwanted interactions.

Use the report option on a post or a person's profile to flag inappropriate content or behavior. Explain the concern clearly and report honestly.

Use Block on a person's profile when you do not want to interact with them. You can manage blocked accounts in Settings. Reporting and blocking serve different purposes; use both when needed.

For safety concerns, contact Muzoi@gmail.com.

Share what is yours

Post photos, videos and ideas you created or have permission to share. Credit inspiration honestly; credit alone does not replace permission.

Get consent from identifiable people before posting them. Do not impersonate another artist or pass off their work as your own.

Respect personal boundaries

Protect your own privacy and everyone else's. Do not share addresses, private messages, contact details or personal information without permission. Never ask for passwords or account credentials.

Private messages and video chat are for mutual followers. A mutual follow is not permission for pressure, sexual requests or unwanted contact. Respect a person's decision to stop a conversation.

Create with care

Share safe makeup practices. Follow product instructions and avoid dangerous techniques, misleading claims or pressure to change someone's appearance.

The AI Makeup Artist offers creative inspiration, not medical advice. Its suggestions may be inaccurate. Check suitability before trying a look and never use AI to create abusive, deceptive or unsafe material.

Be here honestly

Muzoi is for adults aged 18 and over who are legally permitted to use it where they live. Use accurate account information and respect local laws.

No scams, spam, fake engagement or evading account restrictions. Keep this community focused on makeup and creative expression.

✦

Creativity thrives with care.

Content that breaks these rules may be removed. Serious or repeated violations may lead to restrictions, suspension or account termination.
